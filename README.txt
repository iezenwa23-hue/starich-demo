Starich website demo

How to host:
1. Unzip this file.
2. Upload everything inside the starich-website folder (index.html and the img folder) to your hosting public folder (for example public_html).
3. Open your domain in the browser.

No database or server setup is needed. It also works if you just double-click index.html on your computer.

Before going live, replace:
- The three mattress series names and details (Home Textile page)
- The SON MANCAP certificate (currently shows the logo only)
- The LinkedIn link in the footer
