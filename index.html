The closed loop, from bottle to bed

01

Collect

Post-consumer PET is gathered from communities and waste streams.

02

Sort

Material is cleaned, sorted by grade and prepared for processing.

03

Regenerate

Polymer is broken down and reformed into reusable raw material.

04

Spin fibre

Continuous melt-spinning produces premium 3D staple fibre.

05

Manufacture

Fibre becomes mattresses, pillows, waddings and more.

06

Deliver

Finished goods ship nationwide from six regional depots.

  • Closed-loop processing of post-consumer PET
  • Certified recycled content under Intertek GRS 4.0
  • Traceable, compliant supply chain

Home / About

Innovation and materials, spun into better living

Starich Recycling Technology Co. Ltd is a material-innovation company that makes the fibre inside everything it builds.

Our story

From raw fibre to restful nights

Where others buy fibre and assemble products, Starich makes the material itself. Our experience in fibre technology and polymer recovery lets us engineer comfort from the molecule up, sustainably and consistently, at our Aba industrial park.

Today we supply chemical fibre to manufacturers, sell our own home textile range to families, and produce industrial packaging for businesses across Nigeria and West Africa.
